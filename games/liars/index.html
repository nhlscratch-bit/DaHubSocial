How to play

The deck has 6 Kings, 6 Queens, 6 Aces and 2 Jokers. Everyone gets 5 cards.

Each round the table calls a card: Kings, Queens or Aces. Jokers count as anything.

On your turn, lay 1 to 3 cards face down and they count as the table card. Real or not, that’s on you.

Think the last player lied? Hit CAP! The cards flip. If any card isn’t the table card or a Joker, they pay. If they told the truth, you pay.

Paying means taking your penalty: hot sauce, a pie, whatever your player’s got. Everyone has 6 chances and one of them knocks you out. You never know which. The more you pay, the closer you get.

Out of cards? You have to call CAP on the last play. Last one standing wins.
